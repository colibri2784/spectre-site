Privacy Policy

SPECTRE Uploader · Effective 19 November 2026

1. Data the app receives

When the account owner authorizes SPECTRE Uploader through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an OAuth access token and refresh token, and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope. It uses the video.upload scope (and, if enabled, video.publish) only to upload the team's prepared videos to that account and to check upload status.

2. How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in the operating system's encrypted credential store (macOS Keychain). The app has no server and no database; no data is sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API.

3. What the app does not do

It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers, does not sell or share data with third parties, does not use data for advertising, and does not read messages, followers or analytics.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Revoking the app in TikTok's settings invalidates them; the operator also deletes them from the Keychain on request or when the app is no longer used.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by the SPECTRE team.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change accordingly.
